What is MoneyPower? ⌄
MoneyPower is a Web3 Telegram Mini App where you tap to earn tokens, upgrade your power, and connect your wallet for decentralized rewards.
How do I earn tokens? ⌄
Tap the center button to earn tokens based on your tap power. Complete energy and tap power to maximize rewards. Offline mining also earns you up to 3 hours of PPH earnings.
What are upgrades? ⌄
Upgrades increase your max energy, recharge speed, or profit-per-hour. Each upgrade costs tokens and gets progressively more expensive as you level up.
When can I connect my wallet? ⌄
Wallet connection is locked for the first 3 days (testing phase), locked from day 4 to 161, and globally unlocked on day 162 onwards for all players.
Can I earn while offline? ⌄
Yes! Your profit-per-hour earns for you up to 3 hours while you're offline. Return to the app anytime to claim your earnings.
What are social tasks? ⌄
Complete social tasks (YouTube, Instagram, Telegram) to earn bonus tokens. Click to open the link, wait 3 seconds, and the reward is credited to your account.
How do rewarded ads work? ⌄
Watch a rewarded video ad to instantly gain +3 tap power permanently. This bonus stacks with your base tap power for increased earnings.
Is my data safe? ⌄
All player data is stored securely in Supabase with automatic backups. Your Telegram ID is the unique identifier and your wallet address is optional.
